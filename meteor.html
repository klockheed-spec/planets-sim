Orbital Dynamics

• Relative Positioning: The positions of the parent comet and Earth shift annually, meaning Earth rarely hits the exact same path twice.

• Stream Filaments: This variation, combined with gravitational nudges from planets, creates multiple distinct "tubes" or streams of meteoroids over time.

• The "Whip" Effect: Per Kepler’s 2nd Law, the comet accelerates drastically at periapsis (40 units) and slows down in deep space.
The Earth moves 2.57 million kilometers through space every day.
